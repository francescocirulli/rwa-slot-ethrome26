    HOW IT WORKS · ODDS · PRIZES
Fully onchain slot.

    

      
How it works
  1. Scan the QR code with your phone and link your Privy wallet.
  2. Approve a USDC budget for this slot, or use your free spins. Gas is included in free spins.
  3. Spin. The ticket is paid in a Base transaction. After 5 blocks the keeper reveals the result onchain.
  4. Prizes are paid instantly to your wallet by the contract. Every spin has a BaseScan link.
